# Acceptance status

The preview has automated unit/integration, Electron, export, accessibility and packaging coverage. Automated
passing results establish implementation behavior under the tested conditions. Research quality still requires
human review with current sources and actual provider accounts.

## Product acceptance

- Start a bounded question, populate every required section or explain why it remains blocked.
- Keep source attribution and distinguish fact, inference, missing information and conflicting evidence.
- Inspect tables, matrices and diagrams in expanded readers; follow evidence links.
- Clarify through the conversation rail and card threads, including while research is running.
- Stop/pause, reopen and recover without losing the board or silently changing provider billing routes.
- Produce a useful research document/presentation with a coherent summary, analysis, limitations and references.
- Verify all six styles, independent accents and both brightness modes with readable contrast.

## Current status (0.1.0)

The maintainer accepted end-to-end research and ChatGPT plan usage for the 0.1.0 release (2026-10-20). Release
installers are Developer ID signed and notarised. A populated board alone still does not prove its conclusions are
complete or correct: review the evidence before relying on a result. Research runs take time, often several minutes
or longer for broad questions.

## Verification commands

```sh
pnpm build
pnpm test
pnpm lint
pnpm egress-audit --mode deterministic
pnpm e2e
pnpm dist
pnpm e2e:packaged
```

Use isolated test homes and synthetic content. Current task results are recorded in [progress](PROGRESS.md).
Private board audits, local machine addresses, subscriber diagnostics and development screenshots do not belong
in public acceptance evidence. See [release procedures](RELEASING.md) for publication review.
