# orb — product brief

**Research you can see, question and build on.**

orb is a local-first Mac workspace for research that benefits from more than a chat transcript. It brings source
material, structured comparisons, uncertainty and decisions onto an editable canvas. AI agents can gather evidence,
challenge conclusions and build useful outputs while the person remains able to inspect and direct the work.

## The problem

A polished answer can hide missing evidence, inconsistent assumptions and unresolved questions. A long conversation
also makes it hard to return to the original source or compare alternatives. orb gives the research a persistent
structure: each card has a role, agents explain why they added it, and supporting records remain available for review.

## Who it serves

Founders exploring an idea, analysts comparing markets or vendors, students reviewing a topic and people making
complex personal decisions. Templates support company research, diligence, literature reviews, market entry,
product planning, travel and other investigations. A template supplies a research structure; conclusions still need evidence.

## From question to output

1. **Frame the question.** Start a canvas with a question, a template, links or attachments.
2. **Investigate.** orb coordinates Scout, Critic and Builder. Watch cards appear, stop or pause work, and clarify scope.
3. **Inspect.** Read sources and detailed cards, explore tables and matrices, follow links and ask a question on a card.
4. **Resolve.** Compare options, identify conflicting evidence, distinguish unknowns from findings and record a decision.
5. **Communicate.** Compose a cited research document, presentation or canvas PDF; review it before sharing.

## Product principles

- The board remains useful after a conversation ends.
- Sources, assumptions, analytical judgment and unknowns should be distinguishable.
- Agents have scoped research tools and visible progress; users can interrupt them.
- Provider choice and billing route remain explicit.
- Local data ownership and readable outputs matter as much as generation.
- Flat surfaces, six styles and independent accents/brightness keep the workspace comfortable.

## Models and business model

The software is MIT licensed with no required orb account or orb subscription. BYOK connects a provider's API and
uses that provider's billing. Eligible ChatGPT accounts can authorize plan usage through OpenAI's documented local
open-source flow. Plans, credits and account limits are controlled by the provider. Parallel and other optional API
services have separate billing. No unlimited-usage claim is made.

## Current scope and limits

The preview targets macOS 14+ on Apple silicon. It includes persistent boards, browser research, provider settings,
card threads, expanded readers, research documents, PDFs and slide outputs. Long research runs and output quality
remain under human acceptance review. Generated research can be incomplete or wrong; a cited report is not an
assurance opinion or a regulator-approved filing. RFP, investment and other high-stakes use requires appropriate
source checks and professional review.

Windows/Linux packaging, collaboration, cloud sync and a hosted research service are outside this release.
Release installers are Developer ID signed and notarised; see the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) and [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md).
