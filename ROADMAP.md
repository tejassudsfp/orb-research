# Roadmap

orb is an early macOS research workspace; 0.1.0 is its first public release. This roadmap records priorities, not
delivery dates.

## Available in 0.1.0

- Persistent canvas with typed cards, frames, links, search, datasets and expanded readers.
- A coordinator plus Scout, Critic and Builder, with progress, stop/pause and card threads.
- Browser search and JavaScript-rendered sources; optional search APIs and Parallel workflows.
- BYOK provider configuration, per-agent models and eligible ChatGPT plan usage.
- Research documents, board PDFs and presentations with evidence references.
- Six styles, independent accents and Light / Dark / System appearance.

## Done for 0.1.0

- Developer ID signing and notarisation, with downloads by email at orb.tejassuds.com and checksums on GitHub Releases.
- Dependency license review, plus the libvips (LGPL) notices and corresponding source attached to each release.
- A fresh public repository history, reviewed before publication.
- End-to-end research acceptance and ChatGPT plan usage checked by the maintainer.

## Next

- Auto-update for installed copies.
- Deeper reconciliation of conflicting sources, and richer editing of generated documents.
- Faster research runs, with clearer time and cost estimates before a run starts.
- An Intel (x64) build, if it builds without extra work.

## Later candidates

Windows/Linux packaging and platform credential stores; broader accessibility evaluation; further research-quality
evaluations. These require implementation and testing. Cloud sync, collaborative editing and a hosted research
service are outside the current release scope.
