How Orb handles your data ↗
Logs & evidence (optional)

Up to 5 files, 3 MB combined. Logs, text, JSON, PNG, JPG, WebP, PDF or ZIP.

How to attach your Orb log

  1. After the problem occurs, click Choose files above.
  2. In the file picker, press Shift–Command–G, paste ~/Library/Logs/orb/ and press Return.
  3. Select sidecar.log and click Open. Check that its name appears below, then send your report.

You can also drag sidecar.log or screenshots from Finder into the upload area. Review your files for private information before attaching them.

Can't find the log? Open Orb and try the action again. You can still send a report without a log.

    Sending shares your email, report and selected files with Orb support via Resend. Common credentials are removed from text logs; please review all attachments.
