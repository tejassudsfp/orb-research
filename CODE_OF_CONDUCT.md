# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention,
doxxing and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

This applies to issues, pull requests and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content,
close conversations or restrict participation in proportion to the behavior. Decisions should protect participants
and allow good-faith disagreement.

Report concerns privately through [the maintainer's website](https://tejassuds.com). Include only the information
needed to understand the incident. Reports will be handled with care; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where
disclosure is necessary to address immediate harm.
