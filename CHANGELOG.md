# Changelog

All notable changes to orb are recorded here. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/); before 1.0,
minor versions may change behaviour.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-20

The first public release: a local-first research workspace for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.

### Highlights
- **Research canvas.** An infinite board with frames, links, search and typed cards: sources, quotes, notes,
  questions, risks, tables, datasets, charts, timelines, comparison matrices, diagrams, design frames and more.
  Each card opens in a full-screen reader.
- **An agent team.** orb coordinates Scout (evidence), Critic (challenges and gaps) and Builder (comparisons and
  visual work). You can see their progress, stop, pause or redirect them, and start threads on individual cards.
- **Evidence you can trace.** Cards keep their sources, quotations and provenance. Every claim in a research document
  cites the board cards it came from.
- **Deliverables.** Research documents, board PDFs and presentations (PDF and .pptx), composed from the board.
- **Templates.** Starting points for investing, startup and product work, business, research and personal decisions.
- **Your models.** Bring your own key (DeepSeek, Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter or any compatible endpoint, including
  local models), with a model chosen per agent. You can also connect an eligible ChatGPT plan. Keys are stored in
  the macOS Keychain.
- **Browser research.** Public web search and JavaScript-rendered pages with no search API key. Optional search
  APIs and Parallel workflows. Signed-in reading of sites you connect.
- **Appearance.** Six styles (Studio, Paper, Pebble, Postcard, Meadow, Tide), independent accents, and Light, Dark
  or System mode.
- **Private by design.** Boards stay on your Mac. There is no telemetry, analytics or cloud sync in the app.

### Notes
- Get the installer at [orb.tejassuds.com/download](https://orb.tejassuds.com/download): enter your email and you are
  sent a personal download link that works for 7 days.
- Research takes time. A thorough run can take several minutes or longer, and costs depend on your model route.
- Installers are signed with the Developer ID of Fanpit Technologies Private Limited and notarised by Apple.
- If you ran an earlier ad-hoc-signed preview build, macOS may ask once whether orb can read its Keychain items.
  Otherwise, re-enter your keys.
